# CTTN identity and software asset guide

CTTN uses a plain text wordmark—“CTTN” and “Terminal Nova”—in the page UI.
The site does not ship, redraw or alter Nova Ingot artwork. `../favicon.svg`
and `cttn-social.svg` are original text-only SVGs for site chrome and social
metadata; `cttn-social.png` is its deterministic 1200×630 metadata-delivery
raster. They are not copies of the four Starlight Theme Terminal Nova
identity treatments.

For a use that identifies the upstream Terminal Nova brand, consult the
[maintained Terminal Nova brand guidance](https://github.com/Knowledge-Forge-AI/theme-forge-terminal-nova/blob/461d9add96ed6c04b341e31305697a9046eed1a8/README-BRAND.md).
Follow that guidance's strategy: keep approved identity treatments intact,
preserve proportions and clear space, choose the treatment for its actual
background, use only authorized source assets, and do not imply endorsement
or official status. This pointer adds no blanket artwork or trademark license
and does not change any existing legal term.
